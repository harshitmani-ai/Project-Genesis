# DocuDraft AI Validation

## Product Summary

DocuDraft AI helps small businesses generate employee documents such as offer letters, appointment letters, NDAs, joining forms, and other HR documents within minutes.

---

## Validation Goal

Confirm that small businesses are willing to pay for faster and easier employee document generation.

---

## Target Customers

- Small manufacturing companies
- Small offices
- Retail businesses
- Companies with 10–200 employees

---

## Customer Interview Questions

1. How do you currently prepare employee documents?
2. How much time does it take?
3. What problems do you face?
4. Would saving this time be valuable?
5. Would you try an AI tool that creates these documents?
6. What would make you trust such a tool?
7. Would you pay for it? If yes, how much?

---

## Success Criteria

Before building the software, we want evidence that:

- At least 10 businesses are interviewed.
- At least 5 say they would use the product.
- At least 3 are willing to pay or test it.
