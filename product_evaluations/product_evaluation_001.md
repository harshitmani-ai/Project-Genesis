# Product Evaluation Report

## Evaluation Method

To identify the most viable, low-risk, and high-margin product to launch first for Project Genesis, every product hypothesis has been evaluated against exactly six strategic factors. In accordance with **Principle 1 (Profit First)**, **Principle 2 (Customer Obsession)**, and **Principle 6 (Honest Decisions)**, these factors weigh operational simplicity, capital expenditure, and the Founder's domain expertise.

Each product is evaluated out of a maximum of **100 points**:

1.  **Customer Pain (Max 25 points):** The severity of the daily problem. High scores indicate that the problem causes immediate financial leaks, regulatory risks, or major operational delays.
2.  **Willingness to Pay (Max 20 points):** The clarity of the return on investment (ROI). Higher scores indicate that the customer can immediately see how the product saves them money or recovers lost revenue.
3.  **MVP Speed (Max 20 points):** The speed with which we can deploy a working loop. Higher scores indicate that a basic version can be built in days or weeks rather than months.
4.  **Development Simplicity (Max 15 points):** Technical ease. A technically complex system (e.g., custom computer vision or dialect-heavy audio processing) receives a lower score, while mature APIs and simple form-based workflows receive higher scores.
5.  **Low Validation Cost (Max 10 points):** The cost and effort required to run real-world validation tests. Products that can be validated with simple interviews or manual mockups receive higher scores.
6.  **Strategic Fit for Project Genesis (Max 10 points):** Alignment with the Founder's (Harshit's) practical experience in HR, attendance, employee documentation, and small-business operations. Products matching this expertise score a maximum 10/10.

## Top Three Opportunities

### 1. DocuDraft AI (Score: 87/100)
*   **Strengths:** This hypothesis addresses a universal, highly tedious admin bottleneck—drafting personalized employee onboarding documents. With a low development difficulty (3/10 in research), it leverages mature LLM prompt templates and simple form inputs. Its highest strength is its perfect alignment with Harshit's experience in employee documentation and small-business operations, allowing for immediate domain credibility.
*   **Risks:** Formatting errors, typos, or inaccurate details generated by the AI can make a professional contract look sloppy, causing business owners to instantly lose trust.
*   **Validation Requirements:** Interview 10–15 small business owners to map out their exact onboarding steps, and check if they would pay ₹499/month for automated onboarding packets.

### 2. ShiftGuard AI (Score: 84/100)
*   **Strengths:** Solves a highly critical financial leak—attendance fraud ("buddy punching") and unexpected manufacturing downtime. It provides an immediate, easily calculated ROI for small factories. It perfectly matches Harshit’s domain expertise in HR and attendance operations.
*   **Risks:** Factory gates often feature poor lighting, heavy ambient dust, and poor network connectivity, which could degrade mobile facial-recognition accuracy and create bottleneck delays during shift changes.
*   **Validation Requirements:** Interview 10 local small factory owners to verify if buddy punching and short-notice absences are within their top three daily leaks, and test an open-source facial-recognition library on a cheap mobile device under low-light/dirty gate conditions.

### 3. QuoteChase AI (Score: 83/100)
*   **Strengths:** Directly recaptures lost business revenue for hands-on contractors who forget or feel uncomfortable following up on sent quotes. The ROI link is incredibly clear: closing just one mid-sized contract can pay for the service for an entire year.
*   **Risks:** The product relies heavily on continuous integration with third-party invoicing platforms (such as QuickBooks or Zoho) and must navigate strict email deliverability/spam filter rules.
*   **Validation Requirements:** Contact local home service contractors, identify those with outstanding, unanswered customer quotes, and offer to manually manage their follow-ups for two weeks for free to prove conversion rates.

## Recommended Product to Validate First

### Recommended Product: **DocuDraft AI**

#### Why it ranked first
DocuDraft AI achieved the highest score due to its high development simplicity, rapid MVP speed, and excellent strategic fit. It solves a highly manual compliance and administrative bottleneck (preparing onboarding packets) without requiring heavy API integrations or complex hardware infrastructure, making it a stellar embodiment of **Principle 1 — Profit First**.

#### Why it is suitable for the first product
It acts as the perfect entry point because it utilizes text-generation structures that are highly stable, inexpensive to host, and easy to run. Furthermore, because Harshit has practical domain expertise in employee documentation, Project Genesis can confidently design templates that look authoritative, legal, and highly polished, establishing strong initial market authority.

#### What could cause it to fail
If the output lacks formatting precision, fails to match the regional layout expectations of the business, or introduces hallucinated details, owners will not trust the tool with their compliance paperwork. Additionally, if the UI is too complex, busy owners might revert to their legacy "copy-paste" Word document workflow.

#### The cheapest real-world validation test
1.  **Draft a Mock Form:** Create a free Google Form containing 8 basic fields (Employee Name, Role, Salary, Start Date, etc.).
2.  **Manual Generation:** When a business owner submits the form, manually generate the formatted NDA, offer letter, and onboarding packet using a pre-defined ChatGPT prompt layout.
3.  **Deliver the Output:** Send the compiled PDF packet to the owner in under 10 minutes to test user satisfaction and gather direct feedback.

#### What evidence is required before development begins
We must obtain verbal commitments or micro-deposits (e.g., ₹499) from at least **5 small business owners** who state that our manual mock-up saved them significant administrative hours, alongside verified proof that our manual execution took under 5 minutes per run.

## Products Not Recommended Yet

The remaining hypotheses should be postponed for the following reasons:

*   **Technically Complex Solutions (ShelfEye AI, FactoryVoice AI, CompliShield AI):** These products rely on advanced technology—such as specialized computer vision under variable lighting, processing noisy multilingual speech-to-text, or maintaining a dynamic, real-time localized legal database. Under **Principle 1**, building these first poses too high of a financial and time-to-market risk.
*   **Integration-Heavy Solutions (QuoteChase AI, ReReach AI, ReviveLoyal AI):** These tools require robust integrations with third-party CRMs, invoicing software, or VoIP systems. For our very first product, we should avoid dependencies on volatile external APIs that we do not control.
*   **Lower Strategic Domain Alignment (BillParse AI, PredictStock AI):** While highly valuable, retail inventory solutions do not leverage the founder's direct expertise in HR, attendance, and employee documentation.
