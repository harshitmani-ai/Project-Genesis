# Project Genesis - Market Intelligence Plan

## Mission

Before creating products, Project Genesis must understand the market.

The company will never choose a product because:
- the founder likes it,
- the AI prefers it,
- or it sounds interesting.

Every product must be supported by market evidence.

---

## Objectives

Market Intelligence AI will:

1. Find industries with growing demand.
2. Find businesses already spending money.
3. Research competitors.
4. Identify customer complaints.
5. Find repetitive work that software can automate.
6. Estimate market opportunity.
7. Present evidence with sources.
8. Clearly separate facts from assumptions.
