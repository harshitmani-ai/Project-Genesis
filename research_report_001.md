# Product Hypothesis 1

## ShiftGuard AI

### Problem

Small manufacturing companies lose money because of:

- Buddy punching
- Employee absenteeism
- Manual attendance tracking
- Expensive biometric systems

### Proposed AI Solution

A simple attendance platform using AI face recognition and attendance prediction.

Main features:

- Face recognition check-in
- Attendance dashboard
- Absence prediction
- Daily attendance reports

### Why Customers May Pay

- Reduces payroll fraud
- Saves HR time
- Removes manual attendance work
- Low-cost software solution

### Difficulty

5 / 10

### Profit Potential

8 / 10

### Main Risk

Poor lighting or dirty factory environments may reduce face recognition accuracy.

### Status

Unvalidated Hypothesis

# Product Hypothesis 2

## FactoryVoice AI

### Problem

Factory workers often struggle to use HR software.

HR staff repeatedly answer questions like:

- How much leave do I have?
- Why is my salary different?
- Can I change my shift?
- How many overtime hours do I have?

### Proposed AI Solution

A voice-based AI HR assistant that works through WhatsApp or a kiosk.

Workers can speak in their own language.

The AI responds with voice and text.

### Why Customers May Pay

- Saves HR time
- Better employee experience
- Available 24/7
- Reduces repetitive questions

### Difficulty

7 / 10

### Profit Potential

9 / 10

### Main Risk

Background factory noise and regional languages may reduce speech recognition accuracy.

### Status

Unvalidated Hypothesis

# Product Hypothesis 3

- Overtime issues
- Missing skilled workers
- Scheduling mistakes

### Proposed AI Solution

Main features:

- Smart scheduling
- Overtime optimization
- Replacement suggestions
- WhatsApp notifications

### Why Customers May Pay

- Saves manager time
- Reduces overtime costs
- Improves scheduling quality
- Helps with compliance

### Difficulty

6 / 10

### Profit Potential

7 / 10

### Main Risk

Some managers may prefer Excel or manual scheduling.
