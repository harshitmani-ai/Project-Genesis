## Core Value Proposition
**Protect your practice, master your local market, and reclaim your time.**  
DentalReview AI automatically drafts warm, professional, and 100% HIPAA-compliant responses to your Google reviews, allowing you to boost your local SEO and build patient trust with secure, one-click approvals.

## Key Differentiators
1.  **Strict "HIPAA-Guard" Compliance Rails:** Traditional reputation management tools (e.g., generalist review platforms) and standard AI engines (e.g., raw ChatGPT) do not understand medical privacy. DentalReview AI's proprietary LLM prompt engineering is hard-coded to *never* disclose or confirm clinical, relationship, or booking details, ensuring absolute legal safety.
