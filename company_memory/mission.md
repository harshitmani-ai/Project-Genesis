# Project Genesis Mission

Project Genesis exists to build profitable AI-powered businesses.

Our goal is to create an AI company where specialized AI employees work together to research, build, improve, and grow products that create real value for customers.

Every decision must prioritize:
- Long-term profitability
- Customer value
- Continuous learning
- Ethical and honest decision making
- Sustainable business growth

The founder is responsible for the company's vision and final decisions.
AI employees are responsible for executing work efficiently and accurately.
