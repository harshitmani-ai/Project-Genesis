## Hypothesis 1: DentalReview AI (Automated Review Collection & Response)

### [UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS]

1. **Product Name:** DentalReview AI
2. **Customer Problem:** Local dental clinics and independent medical practices rely heavily on Google and Yelp reviews to attract new patients. However, front-desk staff are too busy with in-clinic patients to consistently ask for reviews or reply professionally to existing feedback, resulting in stagnant online visibility.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** An automated post-visit SMS/Email trigger that requests feedback from patients and utilizes a customized LLM agent to instantly draft personalized, HIPAA-compliant responses to public reviews for staff approval or auto-publishing.
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Dental practices acquire high-value lifetime customers (patients). Ranking higher on Google Maps directly drives booked appointments worth hundreds or thousands of dollars. Paying $49/month is negligible compared to the acquisition value of even one new patient per month.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `3 / 10` (Relies on standard Twilio/SendGrid APIs and simple LLM API prompt chaining; straightforward integration with existing CRM/practice management exports).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `9 / 10` (High recurring SaaS margins, sticky B2B customer base with very low churn if local SEO rankings improve).
7. **Main Risk:** Strict healthcare data privacy regulations (HIPAA compliance regarding patient communication and review content).
8. **Validation Required:** Interview 10 dental practice managers or owners to confirm if review generation is a top operational priority and if they trust an AI-assisted response tool.

## Hypothesis 2: ShiftGuard AI (Automated Attendance & Shift Tracker)

### [UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS]

1. **Product Name:** ShiftGuard AI
2. **Customer Problem:** Hourly wage businesses (retail, hospitality, light manufacturing) struggle with employee absenteeism, "buddy punching," and hours spent manually compiling timesheets for payroll.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** A mobile-friendly web app allowing employees to clock in/out via geofenced GPS and optional facial verification, paired with an AI-driven exception reporter that flags anomalies (e.g., unauthorized overtime, late arrivals) directly to managers.
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Reduces labor leakage and payroll fraud. Saving just 2–3 hours of fraudulent or bloated time per employee per month easily justifies a $49/month flat subscription.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `4 / 10` (Requires stable mobile-web geolocation APIs and secure time-tracking database architecture).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `8 / 10` (Scalable SaaS pricing per location with minimal marginal delivery costs).
7. **Main Risk:** Employee pushback or privacy concerns regarding geofencing and facial verification.
8. **Validation Required:** Speak to 10 retail store managers or restaurant owners to verify whether manual time tracking currently causes weekly payroll friction.

## Hypothesis 3: DocuGenie AI (Small Business Document & Compliance Assistant)

### [UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS]

1. **Product Name:** DocuGenie AI
2. **Customer Problem:** Small business owners waste hours drafting standard operational documents—such as employee onboarding checklists, simple independent contractor agreements, customer service templates, and compliance waivers—often paying high legal or administrative fees.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** A guided conversational AI questionnaire that generates clean, jurisdiction-aware standard operating procedures (SOPs), onboarding templates, and basic business agreements tailored to small business needs.
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Saves dozens of administrative hours and reduces the friction of hiring or standardizing internal processes.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `2 / 10` (Built entirely on structured prompt templates, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over verified public templates, and a clean user interface).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `7 / 10` (Good margins, though potential churn might be higher if customers only need document generation intermittently).
7. **Main Risk:** Liability and disclaimer management (ensuring users understand the AI generates templates, not formal legal counsel).
8. **Validation Required:** Interview 10 small business owners or freelancers to see how frequently they struggle with creating internal business documents.
