## Hypothesis 1: ReviewFlow AI — Automated Google Review & Reputation Manager

### [UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS]

1. **Product Name:** ReviewFlow AI
2. **Customer Problem:** Local service businesses (such as HVAC technicians, plumbers, and independent contractors) rely heavily on local search visibility (Google Maps / Google Business Profile) to acquire new customers. However, busy owners and technicians forget to ask customers for reviews post-service. Consequently, they miss out on accumulating 5-star reviews that drive local organic growth.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** A lightweight SMS-based automation tool. Shortly after a service call is marked complete, the AI sends a polite, personalized text message thanking the customer and providing a direct link to leave a Google review. If the customer indicates dissatisfaction, the tool routes their feedback privately to the owner instead of a public forum.
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Higher Google ratings directly increase local search visibility and inbound phone calls. Securing just one additional service job per month more than covers the $49/month subscription cost.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `3 / 10` (Leverages basic Twilio SMS APIs and standard LLM text generation; simple integration requirements).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `9 / 10` (High recurring SaaS margins with low ongoing operational overhead).
7. **Main Risk:** High market competition from established reputation management platforms (e.g., Podium, BirdEye).
8. **Validation Required:** Interview 10 local service business owners to confirm whether post-service review collection is a primary bottleneck and if they would adopt an SMS-first automation tool at $49/month.

## Hypothesis 2: LeadSift AI — Instant Web Inquiry Qualify & Book

### [UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS]

1. **Product Name:** LeadSift AI
2. **Customer Problem:** Small home-service businesses lose potential customers because leads submitted via their website contact forms sit unread for hours while the team is out in the field. Modern consumers expect an instant response; if a business doesn't reply within 5 minutes, the customer often calls a competitor.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** An AI-powered email and form parser that instantly reads inbound website leads, assesses their intent and service requirements, and triggers an automated, personalized SMS or email conversation to qualify the lead and book a time slot on the business calendar.
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Speed-to-lead is the single biggest factor in closing local service jobs. Responding instantly prevents lead leakage and directly increases monthly revenue for the business owner.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `5 / 10` (Requires email/webhook parsing, calendar API integrations, and robust conversational guardrails).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `8 / 10` (High perceived value; businesses will gladly pay $49–$99/month for a tool that recovers lost leads).
7. **Main Risk:** Integration fragmentation across dozens of different website builders (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace) and calendar systems.
8. **Validation Required:** Survey 10 local contractors or home-service providers to measure their average lead response time and willingness to use an automated qualification bot.

## Hypothesis 3: InvoiceFollow AI — Automated Payment Reminder & Follow-Up Assistant

### [UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS]

1. **Product Name:** InvoiceFollow AI
2. **Customer Problem:** Independent contractors and small service agencies struggle with cash flow because clients frequently delay paying invoices. Small business owners dislike chasing clients for money because it feels awkward and consumes valuable billable hours.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** A lightweight assistant that connects to accounting tools (or accepts manual invoice details) and uses AI to send polite, escalating, personalized follow-up emails and text messages to clients whose invoices are overdue.
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Unpaid or late invoices create severe cash flow crunches for SMBs. Automating polite reminders removes the social friction of collections while accelerating cash receipt.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `3 / 10` (Straightforward CRUD application with scheduled cron jobs and template-based LLM generation).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `7 / 10` (Solid recurring revenue potential, though smaller SMBs may churn if invoice volume is low).
7. **Main Risk:** Security and privacy concerns regarding financial data and invoice records.
8. **Validation Required:** Speak to 10 freelance consultants and small agency owners to determine how much time they spend chasing invoices and whether automated reminders would solve their cash flow delays.
