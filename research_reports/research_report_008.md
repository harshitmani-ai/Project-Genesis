## Preliminary Product Hypotheses

### Hypothesis 1: ReportGenius AI
*Focus: Automated progress and activity reporting for small digital marketing/creative agencies.*

1. **Product Name:** ReportGenius AI
2. **Customer Problem:** Small agency owners and freelancers spend hours at the end of every week/month gathering data and manually drafting summaries to "show reports" to clients. This non-billable administrative work causes bottlenecking and delayed client communication.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** A lightweight platform where users paste raw project updates, CSV exports, or bullet points. The AI instantly generates a polished, executive-ready PDF client report and a matching 1-minute video script explaining the key highlights.
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Saves highly paid account managers and founders 5–10 hours per week, allowing them to take on more clients without increasing headcount (aligns with *Profit First*).
5. **Difficulty Score:** `3/10` (Low-cost wrapper utilizing LLM APIs with structured markdown-to-PDF templates).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `7.5/10` (SaaS model with high margins due to low API consumption costs per report generated).
7. **Main Risk:** Standardized output may feel too generic or artificial for high-paying agency clients who expect highly customized personal touchpoints.
8. **Validation Required:** Conduct unstructured interviews with 10 small agency owners to verify if they currently spend more than 3 hours per week on manual reporting and would pay ₹1,500 ($20)/month to eliminate it.

### Hypothesis 2: ShowLead AI
*Focus: Trade show and event exhibitors needing rapid post-event lead reports.*

1. **Product Name:** ShowLead AI
2. **Customer Problem:** B2B companies spend significant budgets exhibiting at trade shows. After the event, sales reps have stacks of business cards and unstructured notes. Compiling these into actionable lead status reports to "show" management and feed into CRMs takes days, during which leads grow cold.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** A mobile-friendly AI tool. The exhibitor dictates a quick 30-second audio summary right after talking to a booth visitor and snaps a photo of their badge/business card. The AI instantly transcribes, structures, and generates a standardized "Post-Show Lead Report" containing contact info, warm/cold scoring, and draft follow-up emails.
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** High-ticket event budgets make speed-to-lead a critical revenue driver. If the AI helps close even one additional contract by accelerating follow-up, it pays for itself.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `5/10` (Requires OCR for business card reading and robust Whisper-based audio transcription).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `8/10` (Can charge a premium, event-based license fee rather than a cheap monthly subscription).
7. **Main Risk:** High seasonality of trade shows could result in high customer churn rates during off-peak months.
8. **Validation Required:** Create a simple landing page describing the product and run a micro-budget LinkedIn ad campaign targeting event marketing managers to measure the email sign-up/click-through rate.

---

### Hypothesis 3: InspectReport AI
*Focus: Property managers and field inspectors generating compliance and site reports.*

1. **Product Name:** InspectReport AI
2. **Customer Problem:** Field inspectors, property managers, and safety auditors dictate or write down site findings, only to spend hours back at the office compiling those notes and photos into official compliance reports to "show" property owners or regulatory boards.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** A mobile-first AI companion. The inspector dictates observations aloud while walking through a site and uploads photos. The AI automatically structures the spoken audio, pairs it with the relevant photos, and formats a standardized, compliant inspection report.
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Field workers despise desk-bound administrative tasks. Completing the report on-site means they can squeeze in more inspections per day, directly increasing their revenue.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `6/10` (Requires a reliable mobile web interface and multimodal image-to-report integration).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `9/10` (High willingness to pay in the real estate, construction, and safety sectors due to clear ROI on saved billable hours).
7. **Main Risk:** Industry-specific jargon or strict regulatory template requirements that the AI must match perfectly to be useful.
8. **Validation Required:** Reach out to 15 local independent property inspectors/managers to see if they would join a free, 1-week pilot program using a manual concierge MVP (where we manually run their audio notes through an LLM to generate their reports).
