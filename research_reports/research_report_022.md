## Hypothesis 1: DentalReview — Automated Post-Visit Review Request & Smart Timing Engine

### [UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS]

1. **Product Name:** DentalReview Auto-Flow
2. **Customer Problem:** Dental practices rely heavily on local Google search rankings to attract new patients. However, busy front-desk staff rarely have time to consistently ask patients for reviews after a visit, and manual follow-up text messages or emails are easily forgotten.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** An automated SMS and email follow-up system that integrates with common dental practice management software (or operates via manual CSV/quick entry). The AI analyzes the type of procedure (e.g., routine cleaning vs. major surgery) and dynamically times the review request (e.g., sending a gentle text message 2 hours post-visit when the patient is home and numb-free). It includes personalized AI-crafted messages tailored to the patient's specific visit type.
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Dental clinics live and die by their Google star rating and review count. Moving from 15 reviews to 100+ reviews significantly boosts local SEO ranking, directly driving new high-value patient bookings (implants, orthodontics, cleanings). At $49/month, acquiring just *one* new patient per year through improved rankings covers the entire annual cost.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `4 / 10` (Standard Twilio/Email API integrations combined with basic scheduling logic and LLM prompt generation).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `9 / 10` (Extremely high software margins, predictable recurring revenue, and sticky customer base once integrated into practice workflows).
7. **Main Risk:** Patient privacy compliance (HIPAA in the US) regarding automated messaging containing patient context or health-related data.
8. **Validation Required:** Speak to 10 dental practice managers or owners to understand their current review-gathering process and verify if HIPAA constraints prohibit automated post-visit texts without explicit clearance.

## Hypothesis 2: DentalReview — Intelligent Review Response Assistant

### [UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS]

1. **Product Name:** DentalReview Responder Pro
2. **Customer Problem:** Responding to Google reviews—both positive and negative—is crucial for local SEO and patient trust. However, writing thoughtful, compliant, and professional responses takes time. Ignoring negative reviews or responding poorly can damage a clinic's reputation instantly.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** An AI dashboard that monitors the clinic’s Google Business Profile, instantly drafts empathetic and professional responses to positive reviews, and flags negative reviews. For negative reviews, the AI generates careful, HIPAA-compliant response drafts that de-escalate the situation and invite the patient to resolve the issue offline without admitting liability or disclosing protected health information (PHI).
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Doctors and practice managers hate dealing with online reviews, especially negative ones. Having an AI instantly draft professional, compliant responses saves hours of stress and prevents PR disasters.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `3 / 10` (Relatively straightforward integration with Google Business Profile API and an LLM with strict prompt guardrails for HIPAA compliance).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `8 / 10` (High perceived value as a time-saver and reputation shield, allowing for easy upsells).
7. **Main Risk:** AI hallucination or poorly phrased responses to sensitive medical complaints that could violate patient confidentiality regulations (HIPAA).
8. **Validation Required:** Interview dental clinic managers to see how much time they spend managing Google reviews and whether they trust an AI to draft responses to angry patients.

## Hypothesis 3: DentalReview — Sentiment & Patient Feedback Analytics

### [UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS]

1. **Product Name:** DentalReview InsightPulse
2. **Customer Problem:** Clinic owners know that patient satisfaction matters, but feedback scattered across dozens of Google reviews is unstructured. They struggle to identify systemic issues (e.g., long wait times, front desk unfriendliness, billing confusion) that drive patients away.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** An analytics dashboard that continuously scrapes and analyzes the clinic’s (and local competitors') Google reviews using natural language processing (NLP). It categorizes feedback into actionable themes (Wait Time, Staff Friendliness, Pain Management, Pricing/Billing) and provides monthly executive summaries with actionable recommendations to improve clinic operations.
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Dental practice owners are business operators who want to improve efficiency and patient retention. Identifying that 30% of negative sentiment stems from long wait times allows management to fix scheduling bottlenecks and retain patients.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `5 / 10` (Requires text-scraping infrastructure, sentiment classification models, and clean dashboard visualization).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `7 / 10` (Valuable for larger practices or multi-location dental groups willing to pay a premium tier).
7. **Main Risk:** Smaller independent dental practices may view analytics as a "nice-to-have" rather than an urgent pain point compared to direct review generation.
8. **Validation Required:** Test whether dental practice owners are willing to pay extra for sentiment analytics or if they primarily care only about star ratings and review volume.
