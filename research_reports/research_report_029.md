## Hypothesis 1: ReviewFlow AI — Automated Post-Visit Review Request & Routing

### [UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS]

1. **Product Name:** ReviewFlow AI
2. **Customer Problem:** Dental clinics rely heavily on positive Google Reviews to attract new local patients. However, front-desk staff are too busy to manually follow up with patients after a visit, and patients easily forget. Furthermore, clinics need to catch unhappy patients privately before they leave a public 1-star review.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** An automated SMS and email follow-up sequence triggered after a patient appointment. The AI analyzes patient sentiment through automated post-visit survey check-ins: satisfied patients are smoothly routed directly to the Google Review page, while unhappy patients are diverted to a private clinic manager feedback form to resolve complaints internally.
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Dental clinics experience high customer lifetime value (LTV). Acquiring even one new patient per month via improved Google rankings easily covers the $49/month subscription fee.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `3 / 10` (Relies on standard CRM/SMS API integrations and straightforward conditional logic workflows).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `8 / 10` (High recurring revenue potential with minimal marginal infrastructure costs per clinic).
7. **Main Risk:** Deliverability and compliance issues with SMS/Email marketing regulations (e.g., TCPA, HIPAA considerations if patient health data is inadvertently logged in review platforms).
8. **Validation Required:** Interview 10 dental practice managers to determine how they currently handle review requests and whether automated SMS routing would fit their existing patient management software (PMS).

## Hypothesis 2: ReviewResponder AI — Intelligent Google Review Sentiment & Reply Engine

### [UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS]

1. **Product Name:** ReviewResponder AI
2. **Customer Problem:** Prospective patients read how dental clinics respond to reviews—especially negative ones. Crafting empathetic, HIPAA-compliant (avoiding personal health disclosures) responses to dozens of reviews takes valuable time that clinic owners and office managers simply do not have.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** A dashboard connected to the Google Business Profile API that automatically drafts personalized, professional, and compliant responses to every incoming review within minutes. Positive reviews receive warm, community-focused thank-you notes, while negative reviews receive careful, de-escalating templates inviting the patient offline. The clinic manager simply reviews and clicks "Approve".
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Saves hours of tedious administrative work while ensuring the clinic's online presence looks active, responsive, and professional to prospective patients browsing Google Maps.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `4 / 10` (Requires Google Business Profile API integration and careful prompt engineering to ensure HIPAA compliance and tone safety).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `7 / 10` (Strong retention tool, though customers may view it as a feature rather than a standalone product unless bundled with review generation).
7. **Main Risk:** AI hallucination or inappropriate tone generation when responding to sensitive medical/dental complaints.
8. **Validation Required:** Survey local clinic administrators to see if slow review response times are a genuine operational pain point or if they prefer handling responses manually.

## Hypothesis 3: RankInsight AI — Local Dental SEO & Competitor Review Analyzer

### [UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS]

1. **Product Name:** RankInsight AI
2. **Customer Problem:** Dental practice owners struggle to understand why competing local clinics rank higher on Google Maps. They lack insights into what review keywords, response times, or patient sentiment patterns are driving their competitors' local search dominance.
3. **Proposed AI Solution:** An AI-powered analytics tool that regularly scans local competing dental clinics on Google Maps, extracting review frequency, common patient complaints about competitors (e.g., "long wait times" or "billing confusion"), and keyword trends. It provides the clinic owner with a monthly plain-language action report and marketing recommendations.
4. **Why Customers May Pay:** Dental practices operate in hyper-local geographic markets. Outranking a competitor down the street translates directly to higher patient acquisition.
5. **Difficulty Score:** `6 / 10` (Requires scheduled data ingestion from public map listings and structured semantic analysis of competitor review text).
6. **Profit Potential Score:** `8 / 10` (Positions Project Genesis as a high-value strategic growth partner rather than just an automation utility, supporting higher tier pricing if expanded).
7. **Main Risk:** Changes to Google Maps data access policies or rate-limiting on public directory scraping.
8. **Validation Required:** Test whether dental practice owners understand and value local SEO competitor metrics, or if they primarily care about direct review generation.
